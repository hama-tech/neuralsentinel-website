Common questions

Does Neural Sentinel send our code or prompts to the cloud?

Absolutely not. Our core differentiator is the Enterprise Docker Appliance, which is 100% air-gapped. It runs local inference within the client’s own VPC or on-premise infrastructure. Zero data ever leaves your environment.

How is this different from standard SAST tools?

Standard SAST looks for generic code flaws. Neural Sentinel is purpose-built for AI architectures, detecting AI-native combinatorial kill-chains (CWE-269), prompt injection vectors, and mapping them directly to legal compliance frameworks (EU AI Act, ISO 42001) that auditors require.

How do we start a partnership?

Step 1: Contact us to receive an NFR (Not-For-Resale) license key so your technical team can validate the GitHub Action and Docker appliance in your lab. Step 2: Once validated, we establish a white-label reseller agreement or technical integration.
